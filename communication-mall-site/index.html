The Firm

Welcome to
Communication Mall

Technology decisions shape how a business works every day. We help you make them well, and then we stay to manage them.

Communication Mall is a vendor-neutral technology advisory firm. We sit on your side of the table: we assess what you have, design what you need, source it across the full field of carriers and suppliers, oversee the install, and manage the account for its whole life.

Our practice is built around the infrastructure every business stands on: internet and connectivity, structured cabling and cloud networking. Because we work across the industry, we can also bring you any service or product a business needs, from fiber and satellite to voice, TV, security and managed IT.

The result is fewer vendors to chase, one point of contact, and technology costs you understand.

The Heart of the Practice

Connectivity, Cabling
& Cloud Networking

Three disciplines that decide whether everything else works. We design them together, so your circuits, your physical plant and your cloud network behave as one system.

I.

Connectivity

The right circuit for every site, sourced competitively across carriers and built with redundancy so a single outage never stops your business.

  • Dedicated fiber and business broadband
  • Ethernet, wavelengths and private lines
  • Starlink and fixed-wireless failover
  • Multi-site and new-location rollouts
  • Bill audits and contract renegotiation
II.

Structured Cabling

The physical foundation, engineered to standard and documented so it serves you for the life of the building.

  • Cat6 and Cat6A copper, single and multimode fiber
  • MDF and IDF build-outs, racks and cable management
  • Wireless surveys and access point placement
  • Tenant improvements, moves and clean-ups
  • Testing, labeling and as-built documentation
III.

Cloud Networking

Networks that follow your people and applications to the cloud, with performance and security managed from one view.

  • SD-WAN and SASE
  • Managed firewalls, switching and Wi-Fi
  • Direct cloud connections to AWS, Azure and Google Cloud
  • Zero-trust remote access
  • 24/7 monitoring and lifecycle management
